# Contributing to the AI-Native Agency Handbook

Thank you for your interest in contributing to this project. This handbook is a living document designed to evolve as the AI landscape shifts.

## 🎯 Contribution Goals
We welcome contributions that provide:
1. **Real-world Case Studies:** Evidence of a successful transition from AI-Assisted to AI-Native.
2. **Updated Tool-Stacks:** New agents or frameworks that improve the "Intelligence Loop."
3. **Refined Rubrics:** Improvements to the AIMS Maturity Matrix based on actual organizational audits.

## 🛠️ How to Contribute
1. **Fork the repository.**
2. **Create a feature branch** (`git checkout -b feature/your-contribution`).
3. **Commit your changes** with a descriptive message.
4. **Open a Pull Request** with a detailed explanation of why the change improves the framework's "implementation-grade" quality.

## ⚠️ Guiding Principles
- **Avoid Theory, Favor Implementation:** We prefer "how-to" guides over theoretical discourse.
- **Maintain the Sequence:** Ensure changes to one Pillar do not break the logical flow of the others.
- **SOTA Focus:** Keep recommendations aligned with current state-of-the-art AI capabilities.
