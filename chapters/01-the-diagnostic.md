# AIMS Maturity Matrix: AI Organizational Scale

## Overview
The AI Maturity Scale (AIMS) is a quantitative rubric designed to measure an organization's transition from traditional labor-based operations to an AI-native orchestration engine. 

The goal of this matrix is to identify the "cognitive anchors" holding an organization back and provide a clear path toward AI-Native operations.

---

## The Maturity Matrix

| Dimension | AI-Assisted (1-2) | AI-First (3-4) | AI-Native (5) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Value Capture** | **Billable Hours.** AI is used to do the work faster, but the agency absorbs the efficiency gain (or hides it). | **Fixed-Price / Milestone.** Value is tied to a specific deliverable. AI optimizes the margin. | **Outcome-Based / Equity.** Revenue is tied to the business result (KPIs). Labor cost is decoupled from price. |
| **Workflow** | **Linear + AI.** "Human does X, then uses AI to polish X." AI is a tool at the end of the chain. | **Loop-Based.** Processes are redesigned (e.g., AI drafts, Human reviews, AI iterates). Loops replace linear chains. | **Agentic Orchestration.** AI agents manage the workflow; humans act as "Strategic Gates" and "Intent Providers." |
| **Cognitive Load** | **Prompting.** The human spends most of their time fighting the prompt to get a usable output. | **Editing.** The human spends most of their time refining AI outputs to match a quality standard. | **Orchestrating.** The human defines the objective and monitors the system's autonomous progression toward it. |
| **Knowledge** | **Personal Silos.** Prompts and "magic" scripts live in individual employees' chat histories. | **Shared Library.** The org has a shared prompt library and a basic internal knowledge base. | **Living Memory.** A unified, agent-accessible vector store/graph that evolves in real-time based on delivery. |

---

## Scoring & Interpretation

### Calculation
Calculate the average score across the four dimensions to determine the overall AIMS level.

**Calculation:** `(Value + Workflow + Cognitive + Knowledge) / 4`

### Level Definitions
- **Level 1-2 (AI-Assisted):** The organization treats AI as a productivity tool. While individual efficiency is higher, the business model remains anchored in labor-output logic.
- **Level 3-4 (AI-First):** The organization is actively restructuring. Processes are no longer linear, and the business is moving toward value-based pricing.
- **Level 5 (AI-Native):** The organization is a system of intelligence. The primary role of the human is the definition of "Intent" and "Strategic Validation."
