# PILLAR 3: RE-IMAGINATION
## Designing AI-Native Service Models

**Objective:** To move beyond "optimizing old services" and invent new, AI-native offerings that were impossible in the pre-agentic era.

---

### 1. Beyond the "Project" Model
In the legacy model, agencies sold **Projects**: a discrete unit of work with a start date, an end date, and a defined set of deliverables.

**The Limitation:** Projects are static. They capture a snapshot of a problem.
**The AI-Native Pivot:** Moving from "Project-Based" to **"System-Based"** offerings.

Instead of selling a "Market Analysis Project," the AI-native agency sells a **"Market Intelligence System"**—a living, agentic engine that the client subscribes to, which continuously monitors, analyzes, and alerts in real-time.

---

### 2. New Service Archetypes
We are re-imagining the agency's portfolio across three new archetypes:

#### A. Intelligence-as-a-Service (IaaS)
The agency doesn't deliver a report; it delivers a **custom-tuned agentic workflow**.
*   **Example:** Instead of a quarterly SEO audit, the agency builds and manages a proprietary "Growth Agent" for the client that autonomously optimizes content and reports on conversion lifts daily.
*   **Value Prop:** From "We tell you what happened" to "We maintain the system that makes it happen."

#### B. Strategic Orchestration (The "Human-Centric" Tier)
High-ticket offerings centered on **High-Context Decision Making** and **Intent Design**.
*   **Example:** "The Intent Architecture Sprint." A deep-dive human-led engagement where the agency defines the strategic constraints and "North Star" objectives that will power the client's AI agents for the next 12 months.
*   **Value Prop:** "We provide the strategic steering that prevents your AI from hallucinating in the wrong direction."

#### C. Outcome-Sprints (The "Hyper-Velocity" Tier)
Ultra-fast, high-impact engagements where the agency uses its proprietary "Intelligence Loops" to solve complex problems in days, not months.
*   **Example:** "The 72-Hour Competitive Pivot." Rapidly synthesizing an entire competitor's product shift and generating a counter-strategy.
*   **Value Prop:** "Speed as a strategic weapon."

---

### 3. The New Revenue Architecture: From Retainers to Value-Shares
If we are no longer selling hours (Pillar 1) and we are no longer delivering static projects (Pillar 3), how do we capture value?

**The "AI-Native" Pricing Stack:**
1.  **Implementation Fee:** Setup of the agentic system/intel-stack.
2.  **Orchestration Subscription:** A recurring fee for the human expertise required to steer, validate, and upgrade the AI agents.
3.  **Performance Equity/Upside:** A percentage of the measurable lift (e.g., 5% of the increased revenue generated by the Intelligence System).

---

### 4. Exercise: The Service Pivot
Take a legacy service and re-imagine it as an "AI-Native" offering.

**Example: Brand Strategy**
*   **Legacy:** 4-week discovery $\rightarrow$ 2-week strategy $\rightarrow$ Brand Guidelines PDF.
*   **AI-Native:**
    1.  **The Intent Sprint:** 3 days of human-led value-extraction.
    2.  **The Brand Brain:** Deployment of a custom AI agent trained on the brand's core values, voice, and constraints.
    3.  **The Orchestration Retainer:** Ongoing human refinement of the "Brand Brain" as the market evolves.
