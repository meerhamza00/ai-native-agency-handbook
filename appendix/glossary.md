# Appendix C: Glossary of Terms

**AI-Assisted:** Using AI to perform existing tasks faster, without changing the underlying business model or process.

**AI-First:** Actively redesigning processes around AI capabilities, moving toward value-based pricing.

**AI-Native:** An organization where AI is the core infrastructure. Humans act as strategic architects and validators, and the primary product is the "Orchestration of Intent."

**Agentic Loop:** A recursive workflow where AI generates, a human validates, and AI refines, replacing the linear assembly line.

**Cognitive Anchor:** A legacy belief (e.g., "Effort = Quality") that prevents an organization from adopting AI-native logic.

**High-Efficiency Dinosaur:** An organization that has used AI to become incredibly fast at doing things that the market no longer values.

**Intent Orchestration:** The act of defining a strategic objective, setting the constraints, and directing an AI system to achieve it.

**Living Memory:** A dynamic, queryable knowledge base that evolves as the project progresses, replacing static folders.

**Time-Value Fallacy:** The belief that the amount of time spent on a task determines its market value.
