# Appendix A: The Anchor Audit Worksheet
## a practical tool for leadership teams

**Objective:** To identify where the organization is still operating on "Legacy Logic."

### Section 1: The Pricing Audit
- [ ] Do we have any current contracts based purely on hourly rates?
- [ ] When an AI tool saves us 10 hours of work, do we feel a "loss of revenue" or a "gain in margin"?
- [ ] If our delivery time dropped by 90% tomorrow, would our current pricing model collapse?

### Section 2: The Behavioral Audit
- [ ] Do we praise employees for "grinding" or "working late" on tasks that AI can solve?
- [ ] Is there a culture of "Efficiency Hiding" (using AI but billing the old hours)?
- [ ] Do we prioritize "The Draft" (the artifact) over "The Intent" (the objective)?

### Section 3: The Structural Audit
- [ ] Is our org chart organized by "Department" (e.g., Design, Copy, Strategy)?
- [ ] Do we have a centralized, version-controlled "Memory" of our strategic decisions, or is it all in Slack/Email?

**Scoring your Audit:**
- **0-3 Checks:** You are likely AI-Native.
- **4-7 Checks:** You are in the "AI-First" transition (High Danger Zone).
- **8+ Checks:** You are an "AI-Assisted Dinosaur." Immediate Pillar 1 intervention is required.
